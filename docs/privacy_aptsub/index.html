AptSub Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

English

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AptSub handles information when you use the app on iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

1. Local processing and no app account

AptSub does not require you to create an app account or sign in to use its subtitle tools. Speech recognition uses models bundled with the app and runs on your device. Your videos, extracted audio, subtitle text, and editing projects are not uploaded to the developer or to a cloud recognition service by AptSub.

2. Information stored on your device

To provide its features, AptSub stores imported video copies, subtitle text and timing, project records, and subtitle appearance settings in its local app storage. Audio files may be created locally while preparing a video for recognition. Exported videos are saved to Photos or to a location you choose, depending on your device.

The developer does not receive this content. Your operating system’s backup or synchronization settings may apply to app data and files you save to Photos, iCloud Drive, or another destination. Those services are controlled by you and their providers; AptSub does not operate a cloud project storage service.

3. Photos and file permissions

AptSub uses system video pickers and, where required, requests access to Photos to import a video you select or save an exported video. On Mac, system file access is used for files and save locations you choose. Permissions are used for these features, not to upload your library or build an advertising profile. You can review or revoke available permissions in system settings; some import or save features may then be unavailable.

If you select a video stored in iCloud Photos, the operating system may download the original video using Apple’s services. This is separate from AptSub’s local speech recognition.

4. Subscriptions and Apple services

AptSub Pro is available through monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions. Standard recognition results can be exported for free. High accuracy recognition can be experienced for free; exporting its results requires an active Pro subscription.

Purchases, billing, and subscription management are handled by Apple through the App Store. AptSub uses StoreKit to request product information and verify purchase or subscription status, such as the product identifier, transaction verification result, and expiration or revocation status. These operations may require a connection to Apple. AptSub does not receive your payment card details and does not send purchase records to a developer-operated server.

Apple handles information under its own Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription.

5. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

AptSub does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs. It does not sell your personal information or share your video and subtitle content with advertisers. Apple may process App Store and system diagnostic information under its own settings and policies.

6. Support communications

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide. We use that information to respond, investigate the reported issue, and provide support. Email is handled by the email service providers used to send and receive the message. Please avoid sending sensitive videos, passwords, or payment details. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence we retain, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

7. Retention and deletion

Local project data remains available until you delete it or it is removed through your device’s app or storage management. Deleting a project in Recent Projects removes its project record and the media copy stored with that project. It does not delete your original video, separately exported videos, or copies in system backups. Manage those separately in Photos, Finder, and your backup settings.

Because the developer does not receive your locally processed content, we cannot retrieve or restore it for you. Keep separate copies of work you want to retain.

8. Children

AptSub does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, please contact us so we can address it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its information practices change. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email tc346492283@gmail.com. For product help, visit AptSub Technical Support.

简体中文

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 15 日

本隐私政策说明 AptSub 在 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac 上如何处理信息。

1. 本地处理与应用账户

AptSub 无需注册应用账户或登录即可使用字幕工具。语音识别使用随应用内置的模型，在你的设备上运行。AptSub 不会将视频、提取的音频、字幕文本或编辑项目上传给开发者或云端识别服务。

2. 设备上的信息存储

为提供功能，AptSub 会在应用本地存储中保存导入的视频副本、字幕文字与时间、项目记录和字幕样式设置。识别准备过程中可能在本机生成音频文件。根据设备不同，导出的视频会保存到相册或你选择的位置。

开发者不会接收这些内容。操作系统的备份或同步设置可能适用于应用数据，以及你保存到相册、iCloud 云盘或其他位置的文件。这些服务由你和相应服务提供商管理；AptSub 不提供云端项目存储服务。

3. 相册与文件权限

AptSub 使用系统视频选择器，并在需要时请求相册权限，以导入你选取的视频或保存导出的视频。Mac 上通过系统文件访问机制读取你选择的文件，并写入你选择的保存位置。权限用于这些功能，不用于上传相册或建立广告画像。你可以在系统设置中查看或撤销可用权限，撤销后部分导入或保存功能可能无法使用。

如果你选择的视频保存在 iCloud 照片中，操作系统可能通过 Apple 服务下载原片。这与 AptSub 的本地语音识别相互独立。

4. 订阅与 Apple 服务

AptSub Pro 提供月度和年度自动续订订阅。标准识别结果可免费导出；高精度识别可免费体验，导出其结果需要有效的 Pro 订阅。

购买、计费和订阅管理由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。AptSub 使用 StoreKit 获取商品信息并验证购买或订阅状态，例如产品标识、交易验证结果、到期或撤销状态。这些操作可能需要连接 Apple 服务。AptSub 不会获取你的银行卡详细信息，也不会向开发者运营的服务器发送购买记录。

Apple 根据其自身隐私政策处理信息。你可以在 Apple 账户设置中管理或取消订阅。删除应用不会取消订阅。

5. 分析、广告与跟踪

AptSub 不包含第三方广告、统计分析或跨应用跟踪 SDK，不出售你的个人信息，也不向广告商分享视频和字幕内容。Apple 可能依据其自身设置和政策处理 App Store 与系统诊断信息。

6. 技术支持通信

当你主动发送支持邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容及你自愿提供的附件。这些信息用于回复、调查所报告的问题和提供支持。邮件由收发双方使用的邮件服务提供商处理。请避免发送敏感视频、密码或支付信息。如希望删除我们保留的支持通信，可通过邮件联系我们；适用法律要求保留的信息除外。

7. 保留与删除

本地项目数据会保留至你主动删除，或通过设备的应用或存储管理功能移除。在“最近项目”中删除项目，会移除其项目记录及随项目保存的视频副本，不会删除原始视频、单独导出的视频或系统备份中的副本。请在相册、Finder 和备份设置中分别管理这些内容。

由于开发者不会接收你在本机处理的内容，我们无法为你找回或恢复这些内容。需要保留的成果请另行保存副本。

8. 儿童隐私

AptSub 不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为儿童通过支持请求提供了个人信息，请联系我们处理。

9. 政策变更

当应用或信息处理方式发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。最新版本及生效日期会公布在本页面。

10. 联系方式

隐私问题或相关请求，请发送邮件至 tc346492283@gmail.com。如需产品帮助，请访问 AptSub 技术支持。
