## English

Thank you for using AptSub on iPhone, iPad, or Mac. AptSub turns speech in your videos into editable subtitles using built-in models that run on your device.

### Contact

For questions, bug reports, purchase issues, or suggestions, email tc346492283&#64;gmail.com.

Please include your device model, iOS/iPadOS/macOS version, AptSub version, and the steps that led to the issue. Screenshots are helpful if you choose to share them; remove private information first. You do not need to send your original video, Apple Account password, or payment details.

### Recognition and export access

- Standard recognition and exporting its results are free.
- High accuracy recognition is free to try, including previewing and editing its results. **Exporting high accuracy results requires an active AptSub Pro subscription.**
- Recognition models are included in the app; no additional model download is required.
- Speech recognition runs locally. An internet connection may be required to load subscription prices, purchase, restore, or verify a subscription with Apple.

### Common questions

#### I cannot select or import a video

Choose a video from your photo library and make sure the original video is available on your device. If it is stored in iCloud Photos, the system may need an internet connection to retrieve it. If AptSub requests Photos access, review its permission in system settings. Also check available device storage.

#### Recognition is slow or subtitles are inaccurate

High accuracy recognition may take longer and use more memory. Try Standard recognition, close other demanding apps, and keep AptSub open until recognition finishes. Choose the correct spoken language when automatic detection is inaccurate. Background noise, overlapping voices, and unclear speech can affect results; review and edit subtitles before exporting.

#### I cannot export a video

Check that every subtitle ends after it starts and that subtitle timings do not overlap. Confirm that enough storage is available. On iPhone or iPad, allow Photos access when requested to save the exported video. On Mac, choose a writable save location. If the results were created with high accuracy recognition, an active AptSub Pro subscription is required to export them.

#### My Pro subscription is not recognized

Make sure you are signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase, connect to the internet, and select **Restore Purchases** on the subscription screen. You can check subscription status, renewal, or cancellation through [Apple's subscription management page](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions).

#### How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

AptSub Pro offers monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions. The App Store purchase sheet shows the applicable price and billing period before you confirm. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting AptSub does not cancel a subscription. For refund requests, use [Apple's Report a Problem service](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

#### How do I delete a project?

Use the delete action in Recent Projects to remove a project and its media copy stored by AptSub. This does not delete the original video in Photos or separately exported videos. Manage those files in Photos or Finder. Keep a separate copy of any result you want to retain.

感谢使用 iPhone、iPad 或 Mac 版 AptSub。AptSub 使用内置模型在设备上识别视频语音，生成可编辑的字幕。

如有使用问题、错误反馈、购买问题或功能建议，请发送邮件至 tc346492283&#64;gmail.com。

请尽量提供设备型号、iOS/iPadOS/macOS 版本、AptSub 版本及问题复现步骤。如自愿提供截图，请先遮挡隐私信息。无需提供原始视频、Apple 账户密码或支付信息。

### 识别与导出权限

- 标准识别及其结果导出免费。
- 高精度识别可免费体验，也可预览和编辑结果。**导出高精度识别结果需要有效的 AptSub Pro 订阅。**

### 常见问题

#### 无法选择或导入视频

请从相册选择视频，并确认原始视频已在设备上可用。如果视频保存在 iCloud 照片中，系统可能需要联网获取原片。如 AptSub 请求相册权限，请在系统设置中检查授权，同时确认设备存储空间充足。

#### 识别较慢或字幕不准确

高精度识别可能需要更长时间和更多内存。可以尝试标准识别、关闭其他占用较多资源的应用，并在识别完成前保持 AptSub 打开。自动检测语言不准确时，请手动选择视频中的语言。背景噪声、多人同时说话或发音不清晰都可能影响结果，建议在导出前检查和编辑字幕。

#### 无法导出视频

请检查字幕结束时间是否晚于开始时间、字幕之间是否存在时间重叠，并确认存储空间充足。iPhone 或 iPad 保存视频时，请按提示允许相册访问；Mac 请选取有写入权限的保存位置。使用高精度识别生成的结果，需要有效的 AptSub Pro 订阅才能导出。

#### 已订阅但未显示 Pro 权限

请确认当前 Apple 账户与购买时使用的账户一致，连接网络后，在订阅页面选择“恢复购买”。可以通过 [Apple 订阅管理页面](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions)查看订阅状态、续订或取消订阅。

#### 如何管理或取消订阅？

AptSub Pro 提供月度和年度自动续订订阅。确认购买前，App Store 购买确认页会显示适用价格和计费周期。请在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理或取消订阅。删除 AptSub 不会取消订阅。如需申请退款，请使用 [Apple 报告问题服务](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/)。

#### 如何删除项目？

可在“最近项目”中使用删除操作，移除项目及 AptSub 保存的项目视频副本。此操作不会删除相册中的原始视频，也不会删除已经单独导出的视频；请在相册或 Finder 中管理这些文件。需要保留的成果请另行保存副本。
